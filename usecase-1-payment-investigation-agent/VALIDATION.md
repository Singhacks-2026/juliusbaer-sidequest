## Output review improvements

A further source review corrected an overstatement: the Swiss procedure says
potential structuring **should** be escalated, and does not specify an immediate
deadline or document-collection sequence. The tool now reports
`compliance_escalation_recommended`, and the agent preserves that distinction.
Observed facts are separated from policy conclusions and investigator judgments.
Narrow questions no longer include unrelated history investigations or repeated
caveats. Across all ten answers, prose decreased from 2,091 to 1,686 words (19.4%)
while preserving the relevant evidence, thresholds, assumptions and actions.
